Mood world

Instead of a simple garden, you have a whole living world — forests, oceans, deserts, and sky that all shift based on your emotional patterns over weeks

Different emotions grow different ecosystems — anxiety might sprout thorny but beautiful plants, joy creates sunflowers and butterflies, sadness fills your world with rain and moody fog (which is actually beautiful, not punishing)

You can discover hidden creatures that only appear in certain emotional states — like a glowing moth that only comes out when you've logged vulnerability three nights in a row

Your world has a day/night cycle tied to when you actually check in, so night owls get a moonlit world

At the end of each month your world "fossilizes" into a snapshot you can look back on — a visual diary of your whole emotional month without a single word written


🌊 Ocean / Underwater

Mental: Write unsent letters and bottle them into the sea


🏜️ Desert

Physical: a hydration tracker themed as filling an oasis — the more water you drink, the more life appears around the pool 


🌲 Forest 

Mental: "Root & Branch" — a visual tree that physically grows based on your mood check-ins over weeks and months. Different emotions grow different shapes of branches. Your tree is unique to you and never resets


Physical: step/walking tracker — every walk makes the forest denser, brighter, and fills it with more wildlife

🌴 Jungle / Rainforest 

Physical/Mental: sleep tracker — the jungle is dark and quiet when you sleep poorly, but fills with bioluminescent creatures the better your sleep gets 

Sounds play in the background and you can choose from a selection of different calming noises.


🌋 Volcano

Pressure and release cycle — combines the mental and physical. You log stress levels and physical activity together, and the volcano finds its own balance — high stress plus high exercise might produce a controlled, beautiful eruption rather than a dangerous one


🌨️ Tundra / Arctic 

Mental: "Freeze it" — record a voice note or type something you're not ready to face, and watch it seal inside a block of ice. No burning, no releasing — it just waits. You can thaw it when you're ready

🌸 Cherry Blossom / Japanese Garden

Mental: A meditation and stillness space — the longer you sit without touching the screen, the more petals fall and the more peaceful the scene becomes
