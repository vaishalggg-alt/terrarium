Welcome to

Terrarium

A wellness journal that turns your feelings, habits, and sleep into a living world that's entirely your own.

Open the App →
Explore

Most wellness apps ask you to fill in numbers. Terrarium asks you to look out the window. Log how you slept, how you moved, how you're feeling — and watch a world outside your window grow, shift, and change with you. No streaks. No pressure. Just a place that reflects exactly where you are.

How it works

Three things in, one living world out.

🧠

Log your mood

Check in on how you're feeling — anxious, joyful, low, calm. Your emotions shape the landscape around you.

🏃

Track your body

Movement, sleep, water, habits. The physical stuff matters too, and your world knows it.

🌍

Watch it grow

Seven biomes shift and evolve based on your patterns. At the end of each month, your world fossilises into a snapshot — a wordless diary of your whole month.

The biomes

Seven worlds, one for every side of you.

Each biome responds to something different — an emotion, a habit, a moment of stillness. Scroll to explore them.

Stress & Movement

The Volcano

Stress and sweat find each other here. Log your pressure, log your movement, and watch the volcano decide what to do with both. High pressure with no release and it builds, darkens, rumbles. Push yourself physically on a hard day and something shifts — a controlled eruption, slow lava and amber light, almost beautiful. It never punishes. It just reflects.

Stillness & Rest

The Cherry Blossom Garden

The one biome that asks nothing of you except to stop. Soft pink petals, still water, lantern light filtering through branches — a space that gets more beautiful the less you do. Sit without touching the screen, let a moment breathe, and blossoms drift down slow. A garden not built for doing, but for being.

Expression

The Ocean

For the things you need to say but never will. Write the letter, seal it, and send it out to sea. The ocean doesn't ask why or what happens next. It just holds what you can't. Some things don't need an answer — they just need somewhere to go.

Sleep

The Jungle

Sleep poorly and it stays dark and still, just the quiet hum of something waiting. Sleep well and the jungle wakes — bioluminescent creatures emerging from the deep, the whole canopy alive with light. Your nights, made visible. Ambient sounds play softly in the background, whatever helps you breathe a little slower.

Movement & Mood

The Forest

Every walk makes it denser. Every mood check-in shapes a branch. Different emotions grow different silhouettes — your tree is unlike anyone else's, and it never resets. The more you move, the more the forest fills with wildlife and light. A living record of every step you've taken and every feeling you've sat with.

Hydration

The Oasis

In the middle of the desert, water is everything. The more you drink, the more life gathers at the pool — flowers, shade, creatures finding their way in from the heat. Small habit, big world. It's the quietest biome, but somehow the most satisfying to tend.
